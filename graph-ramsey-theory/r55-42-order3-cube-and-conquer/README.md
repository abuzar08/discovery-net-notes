# Cube-and-conquer with canonical Z_3-prefixes: no (5,5,42)-graph has an automorphism of type 1^15 3^9

Discovery Net contribution `artifactRef`: TBD

## Statement

**Theorem.** No (5,5,42)-graph (42 vertices, no K_5, no independent 5-set) has an
automorphism of cycle type 1^15 3^9 (15 fixed vertices, nine 3-cycles).

Combined with the earlier contributions (`../r55-42-prime-order-automorphisms`:
no prime order ≥ 11, p = 3 needs f ≤ 21 and 1^f 3^k excluded for k ≤ 6;
`../r55-42-fixed-vertex-lex-leader`: 1^21 3^7 and 1^18 3^8 excluded), an
automorphism of order 3 of a (5,5,42)-graph has at most 12 fixed points; the
open order-3 types are 1^12 3^10, 1^9 3^11, 1^6 3^12, 1^3 3^13, 1^0 3^14.
Not a statement about R(5,5) itself (43 ≤ R(5,5) ≤ 46 unchanged).

## Method

Formula = hybrid orbit formula of the type (`../r55-42-prime-order-automorphisms`,
`hybrid.py`: one Boolean per ⟨σ⟩-orbit of pairs, two clauses per 5-set orbit,
redundant sound cardinality clauses) + fixed-vertex lex-leader clauses (L)
(`../r55-42-fixed-vertex-lex-leader`, `symF.py`) + residual clauses (S) on the
free cycles 4..8 (for each free cycle j the word W_0j of the p = 3 cross variables
between cycle 0 and cycle j is the least of its 3 rotations; W_04 ≤ W_05 ≤ … ≤ W_08
as 3-bit numbers), split into 1576 cubes: one per canonical (5,5)-good
Z_3-graph on 4 cycles (12 vertices), under the group complement × Z_3^* × S_4 ×
Z_3^3 (order 2592), generated by orderly generation (`zpenum.py 4 3`;
Z_3-good graphs on 1/2/3/4 cycles: 1/5/47/1576 classes).

**Soundness of the split.** Let G be a (5,5,42)-graph with automorphism σ of type
1^15 3^9. The induced Z_3-graph on cycles 0..3 is (5,5)-good, hence equivalent
under the group to exactly one cube; the group element realising this (permuting
and rotating cycles 0..3, multiplying all cycle coordinates by u ∈ Z_3^*,
complementing) is a symmetry of the type formula, so its image G' satisfies the
cube. Then rotating/permuting free cycles 4..8 (symmetries fixing the cube)
enforces (S), and finally a permutation of the 15 fixed vertices (fixing the cube
and (S)) enforces (L) by the lemma of `../r55-42-fixed-vertex-lex-leader`.
The hybrid clauses are invariant under every step. Completeness of the cube set
is checked exactly by the orbit–stabiliser count: the number of labelled
(5,5)-good Z_3-graphs on 4 cycles, 2 541 538 (brute force over all
2^4 · 8^6 = 4 194 304 labelled graphs), equals Σ_cubes 2592 / |Stab(cube)|.

## Results

CaDiCaL 3.0.1 refutes every cube (1576/1576 UNSAT; total solve time 2511 s, median 0.5 s, maximum 29.4 s, cube 1205; run with 3–6 workers on a shared host); drat-trim verifies each DRAT and
emits LRAT. The independent checker `verify_cnc_p.py` (standard library; code
separate from the generators) regenerates the formula from its definition and
checks exact equality with `c15_3_9_L4.cnf`, decodes every cube and verifies
goodness, canonicity (brute force over the 2592 group elements), distinctness
and the completeness count above, and replays every LRAT against
formula + cube units:

```

cubes: 1576 distinct canonical (5,5)-good Z_3-graphs on 4 cycles (0 failures); group order 2592; sum of orbit sizes 2541538
completeness: 2541538 labelled (5,5)-good Z_3-graphs on 4 cycles == sum of orbit sizes
certificates: 1576 VERIFIED
RESULT: all checks passed
```

(`logs/verify_full.log`; wall time 2 min 9 s with `--jobs 8`.)

## Trust boundary

Machine-checked: LRAT certificates (against the regenerated formula), cube
canonicity and completeness. Hand-proved: soundness of the split (above), of
(L) (lex-leader lemma, cited artifact), and of the redundant hybrid clauses
(cited artifact). Certificates: 1576 LRATs, 10.69 GB raw (largest 80.3 MB; 831 MB as xz); the
manifest (`manifest.json`, SHA-256 and size of every decompressed LRAT, plus the
cube literals) is stored; the certificates themselves exceed the repository size
policy and are regenerated by the commands below. A regenerated LRAT may differ
byte-wise from the manifest hash if the solver build differs; the checker then
reports `sha256 mismatch` — rebuild the manifest from your own `results.jsonl`
(`manifest_p.py`); the replay result is what matters. The cube literals in
`manifest.json` are checked against `c15_3_9_L4.icnf` by the checker.

## Files

- `cnc_p.py` — cubes (`.icnf`) and residual clauses (S) from `levelL_pP.json`.
- `run_cnc_p.py` — driver (CaDiCaL → drat-trim → LRAT → xz), writes `results.jsonl`.
- `manifest_p.py` — `results.jsonl` → `manifest.json`.
- `verify_cnc_p.py` — independent checker (imports `verify.py`, `verify_hybrid.py`
  from `../r55-42-prime-order-automorphisms` and `verify_symF.py` from
  `../r55-42-fixed-vertex-lex-leader`).
- `zpenum.py` — orderly generation of canonical Z_p-good graphs (p = 3: codes {I_3, K_3}).
- `c15_3_9_L4.icnf` (1576 cubes; sha256 c63a052cc1484581… see `logs/stats.txt`), `level4_p3.json`,
  `manifest.json`, `stats_p.py`, `logs/` (`verify_full.log`, `results.jsonl` with per-cube
  solve times and LRAT hashes — append-only; the run was interrupted by a host reboot
  and resumed, so superseded `TIMEOUT`/`UNSAT-TRIM-FAIL` records of killed processes for
  cubes 1265, 1270, 1271 remain and the latest record per cube is the valid one — `stats.txt`).
- Formula `c15_3_9_L4.cnf` (627118 clauses, 7065 variables, sha256
  22b31916853a0488ff6af7efc9d0ff9ce8cfe334f683502ed22d82144258fc01) is regenerated
  by the commands below (39 MB, not stored).

## Reproduction

```
P=../r55-42-prime-order-automorphisms; L=../r55-42-fixed-vertex-lex-leader
python3 $P/hybrid.py 42 15 3 9 f15_p3_k9.cnf            # hybrid formula
python3 $L/symF.py f15_p3_k9.cnf h15_3_9_symF.cnf 42 15 3 9
python3 zpenum.py 4 3                                    # level4_p3.json
python3 cnc_p.py h15_3_9_symF.cnf level4_p3.json 15 3 9 c15_3_9_L4
python3 run_cnc_p.py c15_3_9_L4.cnf c15_3_9_L4.icnf cnc1539 3 300
python3 manifest_p.py c15_3_9_L4.icnf cnc1539/results.jsonl cnc1539/manifest.json
python3 verify_cnc_p.py 15 3 9 4 c15_3_9_L4.icnf c15_3_9_L4.cnf cnc1539/manifest.json cnc1539 --jobs 4
```
