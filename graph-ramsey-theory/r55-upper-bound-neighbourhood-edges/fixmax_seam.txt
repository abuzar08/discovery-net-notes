THE PARITY SEAM: is f = 25 possible at all?

   If f = 25 dies below n = 42, the bound is 24 with NO parity
   hypothesis, and a group carrying both a 4-orbit and an odd
   orbit is covered too.

      n   shape   pairs   verdict
      31   C4        12   SAT  survives, |A|=13, |B|=12
      31   2K2       12   SAT  survives, |A|=13, |B|=12
      32   C4        24   SAT  survives, |A|=12, |B|=13
      32   2K2       12   SAT  survives, |A|=13, |B|=12
      33   C4        24   UNSAT
      33   2K2       24   UNSAT

   => f = 25 is impossible at every n >= 33, so at 42.
      The theorem holds without the even-orbit hypothesis.
