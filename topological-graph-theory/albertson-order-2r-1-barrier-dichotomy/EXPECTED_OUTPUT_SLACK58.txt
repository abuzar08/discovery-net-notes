How strong would a sharpening have to be?  Priced by re-running the
closure scan with each inequality handicapped.

   configurations scanned: 8313
   closed with no handicap: 325

   Configurations closed when inequality j holds with d to spare:

   inequality       d=1      d=5      d=25     d=50   
   (1) count        3712     3712     3712     3712   
   (2) degree       325      325      325      325    
   (3) Turan        351      424      495      546    
   (4) spread       325      325      325      325    
   (5) S_R-degree   375      587      1816     2273   

   The count inequality is the whole story: one unit off its
   right-hand side closes every configuration the route reaches,
   while the four edge counts need 50 to gain a few hundred and the
   spread inequality is inert at every handicap.

   Read that as a NEGATIVE result about the edge counts, and as a
   precise target otherwise: what is needed is a theorem of the form
   o(H' - S) <= c_A + t - 1 -- the component bound is never attained
   -- holding unconditionally.  Two partial versions of exactly that
   (the singleton refinement of c_A, and the parity of the odd
   components) are already in tuttegen.py and are not enough,
   because each leaves the adversary another branch or one unit of
   freedom in u to absorb it.
