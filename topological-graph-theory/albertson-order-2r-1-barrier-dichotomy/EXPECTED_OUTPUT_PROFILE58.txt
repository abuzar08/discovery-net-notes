CONCLUSION
   BOTH obstructions are close somewhere, which is the useful fact.

   Absorption: the shortfall reaches 1, and 1134 of the 8782
   configurations sit within 2 of closing on it.  It is the broad
   front -- the median shortfall is about 6 -- so a uniform gain of a
   few units in mu_2 would remove most of the case at once.

   Crossing: the shortfall reaches 5, with 2 configurations within
   50 and 54 within 200 of Z(29) = 8281.  But the distribution is very
   long-tailed: most sit 3000 to 6000 short.  So a better crossing
   bound would peel off a few dozen configurations, not the case.

   The two crossing-tightest are single-block configurations at
   m = 840, |R| = 31 and 32, where merging the block with R covers
   ALL 58 vertices, so the bound degenerates to the global
   g(58, 813) = 8276 and the shortfall of 5 is just the row-840
   margin of the deletion-recurrence gate.  Nothing block-specific
   is being used there, which is why they are extreme.

   VERDICT: push mu_2, not the crossing bound.  That is what closed
   order 57, and it is now measured rather than guessed.
