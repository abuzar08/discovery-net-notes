CONCLUSION
   The bound alpha(G) <= 3 was proved and reviewed in the order-2r
   lemma and used only for a Turan cap on the components of H - B.
   Applied to G[L] it caps the whole block structure, because private
   vertices of distinct blocks are pairwise non-adjacent.

   Order 58 at r = 29 is open in 9067 + 307 = 9374 configurations,
   against the 103292 after the two audits: a reduction of more than
   ten-fold, and the first movement in the closing direction for
   several passes.

   It is NOT a closure.  Albertson's conjecture is not proved for
   r = 29.
