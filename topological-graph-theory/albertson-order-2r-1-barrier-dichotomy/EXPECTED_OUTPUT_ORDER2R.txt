PART 3   H has no two disjoint triangles: also impossible
   If every two triangles of H meet, then for a Stehlik triangle T the
   graph F := H - T is triangle-free (a triangle of F would be disjoint
   from T).  Edges meeting T number at most sum_{t in T} d_H(t) - 3 <=
   3r - 3, so e(F) >= e(H) - (3r-3).  By Cauchy-Schwarz
       sum_{uv in E(F)} (d_F(u)+d_F(v)) = sum_v d_F(v)^2 >= (2 e(F))^2/|V(F)|,
   so some edge uv of F has d_F(u) + d_F(v) >= 4 e(F)/|V(F)|.  F is
   triangle-free, so N_F(u) and N_F(v) are DISJOINT independent sets of F,
   hence disjoint cliques of G, and the split bound applies to them.
     m=838: e(F) >= 731, some edge has d+d >= 54, min crK sum 11092 at (27, 27)
