Albertson order 58 at r = 29: the three b <= 7 classes
Z(29) = 8281;  a counterexample has cr(G) <= 8280

PART 1   which classes inherit a usable clique-cover bound
   b=6 c=(51, 1):    |C|=51, H-C is 7 vertices covered by 3 cliques,
        theta(H[C]) >= 26, so every packing has 2t + e <= 25;
        triangle + perfect matching of the rest costs 26  -> FORBIDDEN, transfer usable
   b=6 c=(50, 1, 1): |C|=50, H-C is 8 vertices covered by 4 cliques,
        theta(H[C]) >= 25, so every packing has 2t + e <= 25;
        |C| is even, so C - T is odd and has no perfect
        matching for trivial reasons  -> no transfer
   b=7 c=(49, 1, 1): |C|=49, H-C is 9 vertices covered by 5 cliques,
        theta(H[C]) >= 24, so every packing has 2t + e <= 25;
        triangle + perfect matching of the rest costs 25  -> allowed, no transfer

PART 3   why they survive
   Family A (s = 0, sizes (47,1)): the lone vertex costs 24-1-0 = 23
   and w costs 25, total 48 <= 52, and K_{1,47} forces no crossings.
   This is the descent case -- C shrinks by 4 while theta drops by 2,
   so the transfer reapplies verbatim on 47 = 2*24 - 1 vertices.  The
   descent is self-similar and does not terminate inside the budget.
   Families B (s = 22, sizes (3,1^23) and neighbours, cost 48) and
   C (s = 23, all 25 remaining vertices isolated, cost 25): once s is
   that large a lone vertex costs only max(0, 24 - 1 - s) = 0, so the
   excess filter is silent, and 26 parts give only cr(K_26) = 4724.
   Killing these needs a bound on how big a Tutte barrier of a graph
   with theta(H[C]) >= 26 and Delta(H) <= 29 can be -- not a crossing
   bound.  That is the open problem.

CONCLUSION
   Reduction 1 puts the no-conformal-triangle condition back on the
   table one level down, and Reduction 2 raises every singleton's
   excess by two, but the excess and bipartition filters together do
   NOT close the (51,1) class, and the other two classes do not even
   inherit the transfer.  The three b <= 7 classes remain open.
