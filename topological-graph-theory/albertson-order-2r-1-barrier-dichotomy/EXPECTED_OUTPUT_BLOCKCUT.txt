The block-forest facts behind tuttegen.py, checked by brute force

   Gallai forests of cliques on 3..7 vertices: 6768
   subsets A tested: 719597

   LEMMA   three pairwise-sharing vertices share a common block
           counterexamples: 0 -> PASS
   COR 1   A not inside one block => complement has <= 2 components
           counterexamples: 0 -> PASS
   COR 2   A inside one block => A independent in the complement
           counterexamples: 0 -> PASS

   Both bounds tuttegen.py uses for c_A are therefore sound on every
   small Gallai forest of cliques, and the proofs above cover the
   general case.  The partition case (no cut vertex at all) gives
   the stronger c_A = 1, since the complement of a disjoint union of
   cliques is complete multipartite and hence connected.

   VERDICT: all PASS
