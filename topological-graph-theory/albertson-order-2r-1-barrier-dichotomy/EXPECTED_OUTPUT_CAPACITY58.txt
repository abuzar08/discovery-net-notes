CONCLUSION
   At the point that binds, mu_1 is at its absolute ceiling on 6011 of
   6016 configurations and mu_2 on 5031.  The required bonus is therefore
   NOT an under-estimate waiting for a sharper argument: mu_1 cannot
   exceed min(Za, q_1) whatever is proved about it, and it already
   does not.

   So the scalar absprice58.py found is missing CAPACITY, not slack.
   It tracks |R| - q_1 to within {0,1,2,3} on 89.7% of the class:
   the high set outnumbers the largest Gallai block by up to 19, and
   absorption into Q_1 and L - Q_1 has nowhere to put the excess.

   PRECISELY WHAT IS RULED OUT.  A handicap of d on the left is the
   same statement as a reduction of d on the right, so this prices
   both directions at once.  What the ceilings rule out is the LEFT:
   no theorem about mu_1 or mu_2 -- no sharper matching bound, no
   wider second side, no better defect-Hall floor -- can produce even
   one of the required units, because both quantities are already at
   values no matching can exceed.  The only lever left inside this
   inequality is its RIGHT-hand side |Z| + max(0, t - s), and |Z| is
   |R| - 1 by definition.

   So a successor needs a theorem about |R| - q_1 -- a reason the
   largest Gallai block cannot be small relative to the high set --
   and not a better absorption argument.  That is a different kind
