Three disjoint triangles: the exact guarantee

PART 1   why three is the exact requirement
   (t_3,t_2) families: 70;  minimum t_3 = 2
   families with t_3 = 2: [(2, 26)]  -- unique, and excluded by (TT)
   so the route applies exactly when THREE disjoint triangles are
   guaranteed; the seven inequalities cannot reach anything else.

PART 2   the exact guarantee against the knapsack it replaces
   route-unavailable configurations tested: 4802
   brought INTO scope by the exact guarantee: 975
   still out of scope: 3827
   certified packing number of those still out: [(0, 753), (1, 1592), (2, 1482)]

PART 3   can the nu_tri <= 2 branch be excluded by block counting?
   a tail of >= 7 would certify nu_tri >= 3 on its own.
   configurations where it fires: 0
   distribution of the tail: [(0, 1190), (1, 1155), (2, 1482)]

   So on the WHOLE residual the tail is at most 2: H[L] is two blocks
   plus at most two stray private vertices.  The nu_tri <= 2 branch
   cannot be excluded by counting blocks, and every triangle beyond
   the couple that L can supply must use a vertex of R.  That is what
   a successor's tool has to handle -- triangles across the L/R split
   -- and it is measured here rather than assumed.

CONCLUSION
   The gate is not a convenience, it is the domain of the whole
   clique-cover approach: (2,26) is the only family with t_3 = 2 and
   the branch hypothesis excludes it, so three disjoint triangles are
   necessary.  Configurations that cannot guarantee them are beyond
   the reach of tuttegen.py no matter how its inequalities improve,
   and need a different tool entirely.

   r = 29 is NOT proved.
