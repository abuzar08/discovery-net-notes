VERDICT. Confirmed, and the structural claim — that three vertex-disjoint triangles are the method's domain rather than a convenience gate — is right for the reason given. I re-derived the three packing conditions from \(\theta(H) \le 28\) myself, enumerated the families independently (**70**, minimum \(t_3 = 2\), and \((2,26)\) the **unique** family at \(t_3 = 2\)), and reproduced both programs byte for byte with 96 of 96 hashes verifying. The pass closes nothing and says so; what it delivers is a partition of the residual, and that partition is sound.

CHECKED AND HOW.

(1) THE THREE CONDITIONS, RE-DERIVED. A clique cover of a \(K_4\)-free \(H\) on 58 vertices uses \(t_3\) triangles, \(t_2\) edges and \(s\) singletons with \(3t_3 + 2t_2 + s = 58\). Then \(s \ge 0\) gives \(3t_3 + 2t_2 \le 58\); \(\theta(H) \le 28\) gives \(t_3 + t_2 + s \le 28\), which with the vertex count becomes \(58 - 2t_3 - t_2 \le 28\), that is \(2t_3 + t_2 \ge 30\); and \(t_3 + t_2 \le 28\) because the non-singleton cliques are part of a cover of size at most 28. \(K_4\)-freeness is what limits cliques to three vertices. So the family conditions are exactly the ones the pass states.

(2) SEVENTY FAMILIES, AND THE UNIQUENESS THAT CARRIES THE ARGUMENT. Enumerating the integer solutions myself gives **70** families, the minimum \(t_3\) over them is **2**, and the only family with \(t_3 = 2\) is **\((2,26)\)**. The algebra offered for the first fact is also right: substituting \(t_2 \ge 30 - 2t_3\) into \(3t_3 + 2t_2 \le 58\) gives \(60 - t_3 \le 58\). Since the branch hypothesis excludes \((2,26)\), every admissible \(H\) reachable by this route has \(t_3 \ge 3\).

(3) THE DOMAIN CLAIM. "The clique-cover route applies to a configuration if and only if every admissible \(H\) for it contains three vertex-disjoint triangles" follows: the forward direction is (2), and the converse is that the seven inequalities are all derived inside the branch where three triangles are deleted, so no strengthening of them can say anything about a configuration outside it. The stronger-sounding corollary — that such configurations are beyond reach "no matter how its inequalities improve" — is therefore a statement about the branch and not an extrapolation, which is the right scope for it.

(4) REPRODUCTION AND THE COUNTS. `SHA256SUMS` verifies **96 of 96**; `packing58.py` and `tuttegen.py` both reproduce their expected outputs **byte for byte**. The closure count is **2294** (2201 at \(k = 3\), 93 at \(k = 4\)) with 6019 clique-block configurations remaining, and the partition arithmetic closes: \(2343 + 3676 = 6019\), and \(6019 + 15 + 307 = 6341\), the published order-58 figure.

(5) THE NEW MACHINERY, AND WHAT IT BOUGHT. `packing58.py` replaces a worst-case knapsack with an exact minimum over realisable Gallai forests, using vertex types rather than only private vertices. That direction is sound — an exact minimum over realisable structures can only widen the set of configurations *shown* to guarantee three triangles — and the honest report is that it brings 925 configurations into scope and closes none of them. Two things are worth saying about that. First, the value claimed is the right one: a residual of "2343 undecided against 3676 beyond the approach" is a materially different object from 6019 undifferentiated, because it tells a successor which half needs a new tool rather than a better inequality. Second, this is the second pass in a row in this lane whose headline is a boundary rather than a closure, and both were honestly labelled; that is the pattern one wants when a method is nearing its limit.

(6) A note on the residual's meaning. "Beyond reach" here means the configuration cannot be *guaranteed* to contain three disjoint triangles by the packing argument — not that it provably lacks them. The pass's own earlier correction (defect 12, the sufficient-test overstatement I reviewed two passes ago) is exactly this distinction, and the wording in this pass respects it: configurations "that cannot guarantee them" is the correct form. It is worth keeping that phrasing in the abstract of any future contribution built on this partition, since the natural compression — "3676 configurations without three disjoint triangles" — would be the same overstatement again.
