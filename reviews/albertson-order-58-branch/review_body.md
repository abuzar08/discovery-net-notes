CHECKED AND HOW.

(1) Reproduction. `k4free.py` and `descent.py` at bb36e51 give empty diffs against their expected outputs (79 s and 84 s) and all three sources hash to the values in the bodies.

(2) The no-two-disjoint-triangles chain, re-derived. Every step is right: Stehlik gives a triangle \(T\) in \(H\); \(F := H - V(T)\) on \(55\) vertices is triangle-free; the edges meeting \(T\) number \(\sum_{t \in T} d_H(t) - 3 \le 3r - 3 = 84\), so \(e(F) \ge 731, 730, 729\) at \(m = 838, 839, 840\); Cauchy-Schwarz gives an edge \(uv\) of \(F\) with \(d_F(u) + d_F(v) \ge 4e(F)/|V(F)| \ge 53.02\ldots\), hence \(\ge 54\) by integrality; \(N_F(u)\) and \(N_F(v)\) are disjoint independent sets of \(H\), so disjoint cliques of \(G\) of orders \(a, b \le \Delta(H) \le 29\) with \(a+b \ge 54\); and the crossing number is additive over disjoint subgraphs.

(3) The value \(11092\). My own minimisation of \(\mathrm{cr}(K_a) + \mathrm{cr}(K_b)\) over \(a+b \ge 54\), \(a,b \le 29\) gives \(11092\) at \((27,27)\) only with the recursion seeded by the CCCG values; seeded as the body states, "at the published \(\mathrm{cr}(K_{12}) = 150\)", the minimum is \(10714\). Both are far above \(Z(29) = 8281\), so this lemma's conclusion is untouched; the quoted number is simply not the one its stated seeding produces.

(4) The Gallai-forest maximum. The closed form \(\mathrm{maxgallai}(p,q) = k\binom{q}{2} + \binom{\mathrm{rem}+1}{2}\) with \(k = \lfloor (p-1)/(q-1) \rfloor\) and \(\mathrm{rem} = (p-1) - k(q-1)\) agrees exactly with my own dynamic program over block trees (cliques of order \(\le q\), odd cycles) for all \(2 \le p \le 40\) and \(3 \le q \le 29\); in particular \(\mathrm{maxgallai}(30,27) = 357\). The deletion accounting is sound: at most \(X-Y\) vertices of \(B\) are non-low, each carrying at most \(b-1\) edges inside \(B\), and Gallai forests are hereditary under induced subgraphs.
