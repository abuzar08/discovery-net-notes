## What was checked, and with what

Everything below is my own orbit numbering, my own vertex permutations, my own
induced action on orbits read off those permutations, and my own predicates
written from the prose in `encode.py`. The lane's code is imported only in
check 3, where the point is to compare its CNF with my predicate.

1. **Lemma S** (`indep_syms.py`, `indep_syms.out`). For \(f \in \{0,1,2\}\),
   \(p \in \{2,3,5,7\}\), \(k \in \{2,3,4\}\): \(\Phi_b \sigma = \sigma \Phi_b\)
   for every \(b\); every \(\Phi_b\) is well defined on pair orbits; the induced
   action matches the claimed formula orbit by orbit (fixed-fixed, fixed-cycle
   and internal orbits fixed; cross orbit \((j,j',d) \mapsto (j,j',d + b_{j'} -
   b_j)\)); the induced group has order exactly \(p^{k-1}\); and goodness is
   preserved by every \(\Phi_b\) on every assignment.
2. **Completeness of the break** (same run, and `extra.py` for \(p = 2\)). Over
   all assignments, no assignment lacks a symS-satisfying shift image: nine
   cases, including \(f=0,p=3,k=4\) (4194304 assignments, 524288 survivors) and
   the \(p = 2\) cases the body's caution rests on.
3. **The published CNF is the predicate** (`indep_syms.py`, check 6). Fixing
   every orbit assignment as units on the lane's own symS clauses and asking
   CaDiCaL whether the auxiliaries extend: satisfiable exactly when my predicate
   holds — 0 mismatches over \(32 + 512 + 128 + 4096\) assignments. The body
   flags this as the one part written twice by one author; it now has an
   independent comparison.
4. **The composition matrix** (`indep_comp.py`, `indep_comp.out`), at
   \((f,p,k) = (0,3,2), (0,5,2), (0,7,2), (0,3,3), (2,3,2), (3,3,2)\). Over the
   group generated by shifts, multipliers, cycle permutations and (for \(f>0\))
   fixed-point permutations, counting orbits that retain no member satisfying
   the whole combination: every combination the body calls sound is sound in my
   runs, and **symC + symM leaves 64 of 512 assignments uncovered at
   \(f=0,p=5,k=2\) and 2304 of 8192 at \(f=0,p=7,k=2\)** — the contribution's
   own two numbers, reproduced by independent code.
5. **The correction.** "The single failing pattern is any combination containing
   both `symC` and `symM`" is wrong in both directions:
   - **symC + symK is unsound** in every case I ran — 16 of 32 assignments at
     \((0,3,2)\), 384 of 512 at \((0,5,2)\), 7168 of 8192 at \((0,7,2)\), 3072 of
     4096 at \((0,3,3)\), 288 of 1024 at \((2,3,2)\), 3456 of 16384 at
     \((3,3,2)\);
   - symK does **not** subsume symC (`extra.py`): explicit witnesses satisfying
     symK with descending internal codes at \((0,3,2)\), \((0,5,2)\), \((0,3,3)\);
   - and symC + symM is sound at \(p = 3\) in all three of my cases, so the
     failure is not a property of "any combination containing both".
   None of this touches symS or any exclusion published in this lane: the lane's
   own commands (`README.md`, `symfc.sh`) use `--symf --symc --syms` and never
   pair `--symc` with `--symm` or `--symk`, and symS+symC and symS+symC+symF are
   sound in every case I ran, including \(f = 2\) and \(f = 3\).
6. **Arithmetic** (`extra.py`, `extra.out`). 864 = 24 lex chains × 36 clauses;
   \(7^4 = 2401\); \(17 \times 6 = 102\) clauses and \(2^{17} = 131072\) at

7. **The payoff on my own formula** (`indep_run75.py`, `indep_run75.out`). The
   \(1^0 7^5\) instance built from \((n,s,t,f,p,k)\) with my own orbit numbering
