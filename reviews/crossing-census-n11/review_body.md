(4) BORS Proposition 14.1, checked structurally rather than by name. The nine members of connectivity at most 1 come out with exactly the published \((n, m, \text{connectivity})\) rows — \((9,20,1)\), \((10,19,1)\), \((10,20,0)\), \((10,21,1)\), \((11,18,1)\), \((11,19,0)\), \((11,20,1)\) twice, \((11,22,1)\) — and I verified the constructions rather than taking them on trust: in every case the graph has exactly two blocks (or two components), and **each block is a subdivision of \(K_5\) or of \(K_{3,3}\)** under my own subdivision test, in the pattern the table names: \(K_5 \cdot K_5\), \(K_5 \cdot K_{3,3}\), \(K_5 \sqcup K_5\), \(K_5 \cdot K_5\) subdivided, \(K_{3,3} \cdot K_{3,3}\), \(K_5 \sqcup K_{3,3}\), \(K_5 \cdot K_{3,3}\) subdivided in two ways, and \(K_5 \cdot K_5\) doubly subdivided. (5) No \(V_{10}\) subdivision, and a loose sentence. With my own exhaustive detector — \(V_{10}\) is cubic, so all ten vertices are branch vertices and the at most \(n - 10\) spare vertices are distributed over its fifteen edges, which makes the search finite and complete — **no census member contains a \(V_{10}\) subdivision**, as claimed. The body's reason for the \(n \le 10\) part, that "\(V_{10}\) is cubic, so all ten vertices are branch vertices and there is no room for subdivision vertices", shows only that at \(n = 10\) a subdivision would have to be \(V_{10}\) as a subgraph — which still needs checking, since a 10-vertex census member could in principle contain it. My detector covers that case too and finds nothing, so the conclusion stands as stated; only the word "automatic" overstates what the cubic-branch-vertex argument gives.

(6) The search-space justification. Restricting to simple graphs of minimum degree at least 3 with \(\lceil 3n/2 \rceil \le m \le 3n - 4\) is inherited from the four reduction lemmas at height 2541 and is not re-derived here; the lower edge bound is just the degree condition, and I note that the upper bound and the suppression argument are what make the census a statement about all counterexamples including subdivisions, so they carry real weight and belong to that earlier contribution's review rather than this one.

Two independent routes to the same 87 + 1.
