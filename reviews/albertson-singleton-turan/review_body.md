CHECKED AND HOW.

(1) THE LEMMA, STEP BY STEP. The surviving order-58 class is \(b = 6\), \(c = (51,1)\), so the barrier is two disjoint triangles \(T_1 \cup T_2\) and \(H - B\) has exactly one singleton component \(\{w\}\), with \(N_H(w) \subseteq B\). Since \(H\) is \(K_4\)-free, \(w\) cannot be adjacent to all three vertices of either triangle — that would be a \(K_4\) — so \(d_H(w) \le 2 + 2 = 4 = b - 2\). At order \(58 = 2r\) with \(r = 29\), \(d_G(v) + d_H(v) = 57\) and low means \(d_G = 28\), so \(x_v = d_G(v) - 28 = 29 - d_H(v)\), giving \(x_w \ge 25\) and putting \(w\) in \(R\). Then \(d_{H[R]}(w) \le d_H(w) \le 4\), \(H[R] - w\) is \(K_4\)-free on \(\lvert R\rvert - 1\) vertices, and \(e(H[R]) = e(H[R] - w) + d_{H[R]}(w) \le \lfloor (\lvert R\rvert - 1)^2/3 \rfloor + 4\). Every step holds; the only inputs are the class structure (established earlier in the lane) and \(K_4\)-freeness of \(H\).

(2) THE TURÁN INPUT, CHECKED RATHER THAN CITED. With my own \(K_4\) search over nauty's complete generation of all graphs, the maximum number of edges of a \(K_4\)-free graph on \(n\) vertices is **5, 8, 12, 16, 21, 27** for \(n = 4, \dots, 9\) — exactly \(\lfloor n^2/3 \rfloor\) at each — and the Turán graph \(T(n,3)\) attains \(\lfloor n^2/3 \rfloor\) for every \(10 \le n \le 59\), which covers the whole range in use. So both sides of the cap are grounded.

(3) THE SHARPENING, OVER THE WHOLE RANGE. \(\lfloor n^2/3 \rfloor - \lfloor (n-1)^2/3 \rfloor - 4\) is \(3\) at \(\lvert R\rvert = 11\), \(6\) at 16, \(10\) at 21, \(13\) at 26 and \(17\) at 32 — the published figures — and it is **positive exactly from \(\lvert R\rvert = 8\) upward**, zero at 6 and 7, and \(-1\) at 5. The class uses \(\lvert R\rvert \ge 11\), so the claim "strictly stronger at every \(\lvert R\rvert\) in the range" is right as scoped; the new cap remains a valid bound below 8, merely not an improvement, so nothing is at risk if it is reused elsewhere.
