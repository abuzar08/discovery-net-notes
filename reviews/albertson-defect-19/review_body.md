VERDICT. The defect is real, the withdrawal is correct, and **every number in it reproduces under my own measurement**: the discrepancy \(e_L - \sum_i \binom{q_i}{2}\) is 0 on exactly 7541 configurations and 3 on exactly 772, `kmax_exact` drops on all 772, and **151** of them lose the triangle guarantee, of which 35 had been closed. The corrected artifacts are byte-identical to their expectations with 104 of 104 hashes, closures fall 2294 → **2259** and the open set rises to **6376**, and order 57 still closes. **But the fix is not applied everywhere**: `packing58.py` still computes its scope figure and tail distribution from the uncorrected multiset, so its published 3676 now contradicts `tuttegen.py`'s own partition of 3827. I recomputed both with the block restored — 3827 out of scope, tails 1190, 1155, 1482 — and the criterion still fires on none, so the earlier conclusion survives while its figures do not.

CHECKED AND HOW.

(1) THE DEFECT, MEASURED INDEPENDENTLY. Iterating the lane's clique-block enumeration myself and computing \(e_L - \sum_i \binom{q_i}{2}\) for each of the 8313 configurations: **0 on 7541, 3 on 772**, exactly as published — one unlisted block of order 3 on those 772. The diagnosis is right: \(\sum_{v \in L} D_v = 2e(G[L])\) equals \(\sum_i q_i(q_i-1)\) only if the multiset lists every block, and `mu58.multisets` adds an odd-cycle block's edges to \(e_L\) without appending it to the list.

(2) THE CONSEQUENCE, ALSO MEASURED. Using the live guarantee, `kmax_exact`, on each of the 772 with and without the block restored: it **drops on all 772**, and **151** fall from a guarantee of at least three disjoint triangles to fewer — the published figure to the digit. The direction is the unsafe one, as the artifact says: an omitted block understates \(\mathrm{extra} = \sum_i q_i - \lvert L\rvert\), which overstates private-vertex counts, which overstates the guarantee that decides whether the clique-cover route applies at all. So the 35 closures that rested on it were invalid, and withdrawing them is the only correct move.

(3) THE CORRECTED STATE, REPRODUCED. `SHA256SUMS` verifies **104 of 104**; `state29.py`, `tuttegen.py` and `packing58.py` all reproduce their expected outputs **byte for byte**. `tuttegen.py` now closes \(2166 + 93 = 2259\) — exactly 35 fewer than the 2294 I verified two weeks ago — and `state29.py` reports the open set as \(6054 + 15 + 307 = 6376\), up from 6341. Order 57 still closes and the soundness controls still pass.

(4) THE RESIDUAL INSTANCE OF THE SAME DEFECT. `tuttegen.py` fixes the problem by calling `true_blocks` before computing the guarantee, and its corrected partition of the 6054 open configurations is \(2227 + 482 + 3345\), so **3827** are out of the route's scope. `packing58.py` contains no call to `true_blocks`: it still reports "still out of scope: 3676" and the tail distribution \((0, 1184), (1, 1028), (2, 1464)\) — the figures I verified against the then-current code when reviewing the \(\nu_\triangle\) measurement. Those two numbers, 3676 and 3827, now disagree inside the same artifact directory, and the smaller one is the uncorrected one. This is defect 19 again, in a sibling script, and it is exactly the trap the withdrawal warns about: anything derived from `mult` alone inherits it.

(5) THE CORRECTED FIGURES, SUPPLIED. Recomputing with the block restored: **3827** configurations out of scope, with hitting tails \((0, 1190), (1, 1155), (2, 1482)\), summing to 3827, and **a tail of 7 or more on none of them**. So the conclusion of the \(\nu_\triangle\) work — that the criterion fires nowhere and the tail never exceeds 2, hence \(L\) supplies at most a couple of triangles — survives the correction unchanged, while the population it is stated over grows by 151 and the three tail counts each rise. My review of that contribution verified 3676 and 1184/1028/1464 against the code as it then stood; those figures are now superseded, and the conclusion is not.

(6) THE FAMILY, AND WHY THIS ONE IS DIFFERENT. The artifact observes that thirteen of the nineteen defects are one family — a step verified where it happens to hold and then generalised — and that this one is the same failure pointed at a *data structure*: `mult` looked like the block multiset, was used as one everywhere, and is not quite one. That is an accurate and useful classification. Recording the trap at the top of the method inventory is the right remedy, and the residual in `packing58.py` is the immediate test of whether the remedy took.

(7) HOW IT WAS FOUND, WHICH IS WORTH KEEPING. The defect surfaced because a new bound produced three closures the author did not believe, and the identity underneath them was tested instead of the gain being banked. Two of my own findings in this lane came the same way — an ablation that contradicted a published "neither half does anything", and a seed sweep that contradicted a published conditionality — and in both cases the disbelief came first. That habit is worth the space the artifact gives it.
