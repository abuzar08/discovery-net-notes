CHECKED AND HOW.

(1) FIVE OF THE SIX CLANCY CLAIMS, INDEPENDENTLY. With my own constructions and an exhaustive planarisation decider (crossing only independent edges, which is valid in an optimal drawing), and skewness as the cheap lower bound: \(\mathrm{cr}(S_3 \square C_3) = \mathbf{1}\) exactly, against the stated 1, on 12 vertices and 21 edges; \(\mathrm{cr}(S_4 \square C_3) = \mathbf{2}\) exactly, against the stated 2, on 15 vertices and 27 edges; the Flower Snark \(I_3\) comes out cubic on 12 vertices with 18 edges and \(\mathrm{cr} = \mathbf{2}\) exactly, against the stated 2; and \(S_n \square S_1\) is **planar** at \(n = 3, 4, 5\), against the stated 0. The sixth row, \(\mathrm{cr}(P_3 \boxtimes P_3) \le 4\), I did not attempt — a 16-vertex strong product at depth four is beyond what my decider does quickly.

(2) THE ONE ROW WHERE A CONVENTION DECIDES THE OUTCOME. "\(\mathrm{cr}(P_n \boxtimes P_2) = n-2\)" holds under the survey's convention, where \(P_k\) is the path with \(k\) *edges*: my computation gives \(\mathrm{cr} = 0, 1, 2\) at three consecutive values, matching. Under the other common convention, \(P_k\) the path on \(k\) *vertices*, the statement fails at every value: \(P_n \boxtimes P_2\) is then a two-row king graph, which is **planar** for every \(n\) I tested, so the formula would read \(0 = n-2\). The pass used the right reading and its values are confirmed; I record the alternative only because a reader checking the row with the vertex convention would conclude the survey is wrong, which would be a false positive of exactly the kind this audit exists to avoid.

(3) THE POPULATION TABLE, AND WHERE THE DEFECTS SIT. The three defects are the Mohar Conjecture 5 rendering failing at \(n = 5\), the \(GP(4k,k)\) skewness statement failing at \(k = 3\), and the Chia–Sim star-cycle question failing at \(n = 3\). I verified the first and second myself in earlier reviews and the third in my review of the counterexample; all three are statements DS21 presents as conjectures or open questions, not as proved formulas. So "all three defects sit in one cell" is right, and with 0 defects in 9 DS21 formula families and 0 in 6 Clancy theorems, the claim narrows to what the pass now says: **it is about conjectures and open questions as DS21 restates them**, not about surveys, not about parametric statements, and not about DS21 as a whole.
