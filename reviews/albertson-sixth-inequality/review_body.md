CHECKED AND HOW.

(1) INEQUALITY (6), DERIVED INDEPENDENTLY. At order \(58 = 2r\) with \(r = 29\) one has \(x_z = 29 - d_H(z)\), so \(\sum_{z \in U} d_H(z) = 29u - \sum_{z \in U} x_z\). Every \(z \in R\) has \(x_z \ge 1\) and \(\sum_{z \in R} x_z = X = 2m - 1624\), so the omitted terms cost at least \(\lvert R\rvert - u\) and \(\sum_{z \in U} d_H(z) \ge 29u - X + \lvert R\rvert - u = 28u + \lvert R\rvert - X\); if \(w \notin U\) one omitted term is at least 25 instead of 1, adding 24. I checked the algebra at sample points and it is identical to the stated form. On the other side, \(U\) is *defined* as the union of the components of \(H' - S\) containing no vertex of \(A\), so a \(U\)-vertex has no \(A\)-neighbour by construction — not by assumption — and its neighbours lie in \(S_L\), in the \(3k\) deleted triangle vertices, in \(S_R\), or inside its own component; different components of \(H' - S\) are non-adjacent, so nothing else is reachable. Hence \(\sum_{z \in U} d_H(z) \le u(s_L + 3k + s_R) + \sum_i 2\,\mathrm{tur}(n_i)\) over the \(t\) components.

(2) THE ONE STEP WORTH CHECKING. Replacing \(\sum_i \mathrm{tur}(n_i)\) by \(\mathrm{tur}(u - t + 1)\) needs that concentrating the vertices in one component maximises the Turán sum. I verified by dynamic programme that \(\max \sum_i \lfloor n_i^2/3 \rfloor\) over \(t\) positive parts summing to \(u\) is exactly \(\lfloor (u-t+1)^2/3 \rfloor\) for **every** \(1 \le t \le u \le 32\), which covers the whole parameter range. And \(X = 2m - 1624\) is 52, 54, 56 at \(m = 838, 839, 840\), so the assumption \(X \le 56\) holds with equality only at the top row.

(7) The by-product. Sound as stated.
