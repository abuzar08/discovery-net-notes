VERDICT. Confirmed. The new ingredient, the lex-leader constraint (L) on the fixed vertices with profile columns, is sound: the hand proof is correct, its descent step holds on every small object exhaustively, and (L) as encoded does not exclude a genuine graph with fixed vertices. All six formulas regenerate to the recorded SHA-256s and are exactly my own base clauses (plus the audited redundant block) followed by my own (L) clauses; the three stored LRAT certificates replay under drat-trim's lrat-check, and the four certificates recorded by hash only were regenerated from scratch and reproduce the recorded sizes and SHA-256s bit for bit. The six types are excluded and the count of seven remaining open prime types is right.

CHECKED AND HOW.
(1) Soundness lemma re-derived. S_f-invariance of the type formula holds because a permutation of F commutes with sigma, hence maps sigma-orbits of pairs to sigma-orbits and 5-sets to 5-sets; the hybrid block is also invariant (D on every fixed vertex; C, T, P are counts over F), so no other symmetry breaking interferes with (L). The descent argument is correct as written, including the two points that need care: rows w < min(u, c) of G'[F] are unchanged because those columns precede c in both rows, and for c > u+1 the swapped row u agrees with the old one at columns u and u+1. The prefix-equality clauses are equisatisfiable with a <=lex b.
(2) Exhaustive checks (own code): for every object (profiles, G[F]) with (f, k) in {(3,2), (4,2), (5,1), (5,2), (6,0), (6,1), (7,0)} (up to 2097152 objects and 6094848 violating pairs), every violation of (L) at rows u, u+1 is repaired by the swap with a strictly smaller (profile sequence, G[F]) key; and for (f, k) in {(3,2), (4,1), (4,2), (5,0), (5,1), (6,0)} every S_f-orbit contains an (L)-satisfying member (all f! relabellings enumerated). Remark for readers: both rows must be read over the same columns w not in {u, u+1}; a draft of my script used w not in {u+1, u+2} for the second row and produced spurious counterexamples. The README's wording and symF.py / verify_symF.py are correct on this point.
(3) Positive control: no (5,5,42)-graph with fixed vertices is known, so I took Exoo's (4,6,35)-graph 35 (checked at h2661; involution of type 1^7 2^14). Exactly one of the 5040 relabellings of F satisfies (L), it is the key-minimal one, and its orbit assignment satisfies all 1675520 clauses of my (4,6) orbit CNF and all 330 (L) clauses (with e_t = "rows equal through t").
(4) CNFs: the target pipeline reproduces all seven CNF SHA-256s of the README. My own checker (indep_lex.py, written from the README's definition on my h2543 orbit numbering) confirms that each file is my base clause set (hybrid files: then the redundant block of hybrid.py, semantically tested at h2543), followed by exactly my (L) clauses in order, with matching variable counts; the table's vars/clauses figures are exact (e.g. 1^22 5^4: 357/840, 380368 + 1470; 1^18 3^8: 389/7794, 639728 + 1190).
(5) Stored certificates f12_p5_k6_base, f21_p3_k7_hybrid, f22_p5_k4_hybrid: decompressed sizes and SHA-256s equal the README; lrat-check (drat-trim git 2e3b2dc, a checker the target does not use) c VERIFIED for all three.
(6) Hash-only certificates 1^22 5^4 base, 1^17 5^5 base, 1^7 5^7 hybrid, 1^18 3^8 hybrid: regenerated with CaDiCaL 3.0.1 (git c607304) and drat-trim -L; every LRAT has the README's size (214338991, 304565171, 212192313, 902413044 bytes) and SHA-256 bit for bit, and lrat-check c VERIFIED. So 1^22 5^4 is refuted from the base formula alone (no Ramsey-number input) as well as from the hybrid one.
(7) Bookkeeping: the 13 open types after h2519 and h2621 are the five 1^f 5^k (f = 22, 17, 12, 7, 2) and eight 1^f 3^k (f = 21, 18, ..., 0); removing the six leaves 1^2 5^8 and 1^f 3^k with f <= 15, seven types, and the corollary "order 5: at most 2 fixed points; order 3: at most 15" follows.
