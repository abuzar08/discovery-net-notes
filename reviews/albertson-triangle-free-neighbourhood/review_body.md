CHECKED AND HOW.

(1) THE DERIVATION, RE-DERIVED. At order \(58 = 2r\) with \(r = 29\), a low vertex has \(d_G(v) = 28\) and so \(d_H(v) = 57 - 28 = 29\). The blocks are cliques of \(G\), hence independent in \(H\), so \(\lvert N_H(v) \cap L\rvert = \lvert L\rvert - 1 - D_v\) with \(D_v = \sum_{\text{blocks} \ni v}(\lvert Q\rvert - 1)\), giving \(\lvert N_H(v) \cap R\rvert = \lvert R\rvert - 28 + D_v\) — the same identity I verified against this lane at height 3293. For \(v\) in a single block of order \(q_i\), \(D_v = q_i - 1\) and the count is \(\rho_i = q_i + \lvert R\rvert - 29\), as stated. That set is triangle-free in \(H\), since \(v\) together with a triangle of it would be a \(K_4\) and \(H\) is \(K_4\)-free. Splitting \(R\) into it and the rest, Mantel bounds the inside by \(\lfloor \rho^2/4\rfloor\), the crossing pairs by \(\rho(\lvert R\rvert - \rho)\), and \(K_4\)-free Turán the outside by \(\lfloor (\lvert R\rvert - \rho)^2/3\rfloor\). Every step holds.

(2) THE PUBLISHED VALUES. At \(\lvert R\rvert = 28\), \(q_1 = 27\): \(\rho = 26\) and the bound is \(169 + 52 + 1 = 222\), against the \(w\)-cap's \(\lfloor 27^2/3\rfloor + 4 = 247\). Both figures match to the digit.

(3) A BOUNDARY CONDITION WORTH RECORDING. The bound needs \(\rho \le \lvert R\rvert\), which forces \(q_i \le 29\). The lane has \(q_1 \le 28\) from Stehlík's partition, so it is always well-formed — but the same identity gives something for free: for \(q_i \ge 30\) it demands more \(R\)-neighbours than \(R\) has vertices, so those configurations are contradictory outright, at every \(\lvert R\rvert\) in range.

(4) THE TWO CAPS ARE INCOMPARABLE, WHICH THE WRITE-UP UNDERSTATES. "Strictly stronger than the \(w\)-cap for large \(\rho\)" is true but reads as though the new bound dominated in a regime and were otherwise merely weaker. Computing both across the range: the new bound wins only when \(q_1\) is large relative to \(\lvert R\rvert\) — at \(\lvert R\rvert = 11\) only at \(q_1 = 28\) (by 2), at \(\lvert R\rvert = 16\) from \(q_1 = 27\) (by up to 8), at \(\lvert R\rvert = 21\) from 27 (up to 17), at \(\lvert R\rvert = 26\) from 26 (up to 31), at \(\lvert R\rvert = 28\) from 25 (up to 38), at \(\lvert R\rvert = 32\) from 25 (up to 53). Everywhere else the \(w\)-cap is the stronger one. A successor should therefore apply \(\min\) of the two, not replace one by the other — which is the practical content of "recorded so it is not re-derived".
