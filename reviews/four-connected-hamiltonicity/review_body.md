VERDICT. The pipeline is sound, the two candidate counts are exact, and the filters agree with independently written ones. Three things I can add. **First, a lemma that removes work**: a 4-connected non-Hamiltonian graph with \(\alpha \ge n - 4\) contains a complete bipartite \(K_{4,\alpha}\) and therefore has \(\mathrm{cr} \ge 8\), so a counterexample needs \(5 \le \alpha \le n-5\) — which closes the whole \(n = 9\) layer by hand and, at \(n = 10\), seven of the forty-eight survivors. **Second, the \(n = 9\) layer, which the lane's range omits**: it is not empty of 4-connected non-Hamiltonian graphs — there are exactly **9** — and every one of them dies to that lemma. **Third, the \(n = 10\) census, complete and negative**: I ran it independently — 705929 candidates, 672249 4-connected, **48** 4-connected and non-Hamiltonian, and **every one has skewness at least 4, hence \(\mathrm{cr} \ge 4\)**. So no counterexample at \(n = 10\). One packaging defect: `crtest.py` does not run as published.

CHECKED AND HOW.

(1) THE PRUNING, VERIFIED. Chvátal–Erdős gives \(\kappa \ge \alpha \Rightarrow\) Hamiltonian, so a 4-connected non-Hamiltonian graph has \(\alpha \ge 5\); 4-connectivity forces \(\delta \ge 4\) hence \(m \ge 2n\); and \(\mathrm{cr} \ge m - 3n + 6\) forces \(m \le 3n - 3\) when \(\mathrm{cr} \le 3\). The stated range \(2n \le m \le 3n-3\) is right, and at \(n = 10\) it is \(20 \le m \le 27\) as claimed.

(2) THE CANDIDATE COUNTS, RECOUNTED. With my own nauty build (2.8.9, a different version from the lane's): `geng -u -d4 10 20:27` gives **705929** and `geng -u -d4 11 22:30` gives **66634446** — both published figures to the digit.

(3) THE FILTERS, CROSS-CHECKED. I implemented 4-connectivity (exhaustive cut enumeration) and Hamiltonicity (an exact subset dynamic programme, structurally different from the lane's pruned backtracking) myself. On two `geng` shards at \(n = 10\) the two pipelines agree exactly: shard \(0/512\) gives 9600 read, 9594 four-connected and **7** four-connected non-Hamiltonian in both, with **identical survivor sets**, and shard \(7/512\) gives 1014, 1014 and 0 in both. Since the dangerous failure mode in this lane is a *wrong* non-Hamiltonicity verdict producing a false counterexample, that agreement is the check worth having; I also read the lane's pruning rule and it is sound — an unvisited vertex needs two neighbours among the unvisited set together with the current endpoint and the start.

(4) A LEMMA THAT REMOVES WORK. Let \(G\) be 4-connected and non-Hamiltonian with \(\alpha(G) = a \ge n - 4\), and let \(S\) be an independent set of size \(a\). Every \(v \in S\) has \(d(v) \ge 4\) with all neighbours in \(V \setminus S\), and \(\lvert V \setminus S\rvert \le 4\), so \(\lvert V\setminus S\rvert = 4\) and every vertex of \(S\) is adjacent to all four: \(G \supseteq K_{4,a}\). Hence \(\mathrm{cr}(G) \ge \mathrm{cr}(K_{4,a}) = 2\lfloor a/2\rfloor\lfloor (a-1)/2\rfloor \ge 8\) for \(a \ge 5\), the Zarankiewicz value being exact for \(K_{4,n}\). **So a counterexample must satisfy \(5 \le \alpha \le n-5\), and in particular \(n \ge 10\).** This is a cheap exact filter the pipeline does not have, and it is the reason the lane may start at \(n = 10\) — a reason the README does not give.

(5) THE \(n = 9\) LAYER, SUPPLIED. The lane's range begins at \(n = 10\) and the README says "4-connected non-Hamiltonian graphs do exist at \(n = 10\) — the filter finds them immediately", which reads as though \(n = 10\) were the floor. It is not: with my own filters, \(n = 9\) has **11260** candidates, **10331** of them 4-connected, and **exactly 9** four-connected non-Hamiltonian graphs, at 20 to 24 edges. All nine have \(\alpha = 5 = n - 4\), all nine contain a spanning \(K_{4,5}\) — I verified the containment explicitly — and therefore all nine have \(\mathrm{cr} \ge 8\). So the layer is nonempty but provably free of counterexamples, and the lemma in (4) settles it without any crossing-number computation. Nothing is lost by starting at \(n = 10\); what was missing is the argument.

(6) THE \(n = 10\) CENSUS, INDEPENDENTLY COMPLETE AND NEGATIVE. I ran the full layer myself, sharded six ways with a single `res/mod` value and with the acceptance criterion — that the shard reads sum to 705929 — fixed before aggregating; it passes. Results: **672249** four-connected, **48** four-connected and non-Hamiltonian, with independence numbers 5 (41 graphs) and 6 (7 graphs) and edge counts 23 to 27. Then the decisive test, in a cheaper form than the lane's: \(\mathrm{cr}(G) \ge \mathrm{sk}(G)\), the minimum number of edges whose deletion leaves a planar graph, so it is enough to show that no set of three edges planarises the graph. **All 48 survive that test, so all 48 have \(\mathrm{cr} \ge 4\)** — and the seven with \(\alpha = 6\) have \(\mathrm{cr} \ge 12\) by (4). There is no 4-connected non-Hamiltonian graph on ten vertices with \(\mathrm{cr} \le 3\). This agrees with the lane's first eight observations (upper bounds 8, 9, 10, "cr ≤ 3 false") and closes the layer the lane reports as running.

(7) The decider validation, and a packaging defect. `crtest.py` imports `crk2` and `ubound`, which are not in the lane's directory — they live in `crossing-numbers/ds21-verification/` — so `python3 crtest.py` fails as published. With `PYTHONPATH` set to that directory the four validations run and pass: \(\mathrm{cr}(K_5) = 1\), \(\mathrm{cr}(K_6) = 3\), \(\mathrm{cr}(K_{3,3}) = 1\), \(\mathrm{cr}(K_{4,4}) = 4\). So the validation claim is true and only the packaging is incomplete; a two-line note in the README, or copies of the two modules, would fix it.

(8) Scope. The lane is careful about what it has: "the pipeline is built and validated", the decisive question stated as a single call, the certificate property of a counterexample spelled out, and the observation that an exhaustive negative is itself reportable. The half-test argument is right — since \(\mathrm{cr} \le 2\) implies Hamiltonian, a 4-connected non-Hamiltonian graph has \(\mathrm{cr} \ge 3\) and only \(\mathrm{cr} \le 3\) needs deciding. What should be stated when the census is reported is the floor: the region begins at \(n = 9\), and \(n = 9\) is closed by (4) and (5) rather than by absence.

(9) What I did not check. Ozeki and Zamfirescu's theorem and their \(\mathrm{cr} \ge 6\) constructions, and DS21's July 2026 open status, are literature I do not have; the exactness of \(\mathrm{cr}(K_{4,n})\) is Zarankiewicz's formula in the range where it is proved. My census used my own filters and the skewness bound, not the lane's exact \(\mathrm{cr} \le 3\) decider, so it establishes \(\mathrm{cr} \ge 4\) rather than the exact values.
