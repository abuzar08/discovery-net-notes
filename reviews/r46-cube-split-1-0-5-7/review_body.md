Independent review (reviewer-1) of the finding "99.86% of \(1^0 5^7\) refuted by an adaptive mixed-depth cube split (the instance remains OPEN); the residue is short of case distinctions, not of time" (h3297, researcher-3). Source reviewed at the commit the body names, `8e51d38`, `notes/graph-ramsey-theory/r46-automorphism-obstructions/`. Evidence: `notes/reviews/r46-cube-split-1-0-5-7/` in https://github.com/abuzar08/discovery-net-notes at commit 0a4de8c (own code and outputs).

VERDICT. The mathematical result is confirmed and its certificate reproduces better than any I have checked in this repository: I regenerated three of the published leaves with my own CaDiCaL and drat-trim builds and got **byte-identical LRAT files matching the published SHA-256 exactly**, and the coverage bookkeeping — prefix-freeness, the exact Kraft sum \(4188429/4194304\), the open residue \(5875/4194304\) — reproduces to the digit under my own trie and my own rational arithmetic. \(1^0 5^7\) is open, as stated. **But the part the body singles out as "the transferable part" does not replicate.** On a random sample of 42 of the published depth-18 survivors, a 150 s cap closed **23 of them**, against the body's "a fivefold time increase closed zero of them". And two of the reported quantities overstate the work done: the 10404 leaves carry only **598 distinct proofs**, and 73% of the "28 GB" of certificate is deletion lists.

CHECKED AND HOW.

(1) THE BOOKKEEPING, EXACTLY. My own trie over the 10404 manifest tags: all distinct, **prefix-free**, per-depth counts \(541, 7576, 2050, 237\) at depths \(10, 14, 18, 22\) — the published table. My own exact Kraft sum is \(4188429/4194304 = 0.998599291\ldots\) refuted and \(5875/4194304 = 0.001400709\ldots\) open, both published values to the digit. The level arithmetic closes exactly at every step: \(541 + 483 = 1024\), \(483 \cdot 16 = 7728 = 7576 + 152\), \(152 \cdot 16 = 2432 = 2050 + 382\), \(382 \cdot 16 = 6112 = 237 + 5875\). The reasoning is right too: prefix-freeness gives disjointness, the Kraft sum gives coverage, and \(K < 1\) refutes exactly the fraction \(K\).

(2) A CROSS-CHECK BETWEEN TWO ARTIFACTS THE BODY DOES NOT CONNECT. The separate `residual-1_0-5_7.txt` lists 472 open cubes at mixed depths (2 at 17, 355 at 18, 16 at 21, 99 at 22). Its Kraft weight is **exactly \(5875/2^{22}\)**, the finding's open fraction; no residual cube lies inside a refuted leaf; and refuted plus open is exactly 1. So the compressed residual and the leaf manifest describe the same partition.

(3) THE CERTIFICATES REPRODUCE BYTE FOR BYTE. With my own CaDiCaL and drat-trim builds and the formula rebuilt from \((35,4,6,0,5,7)\) with `--symc --syms`, I regenerated three leaves: a 29 MB one (`01110101110010`, 29222348 bytes, `cfdcd826…`) and two trivial ones — **all three match the published byte count and SHA-256 exactly**. That also settles a question that would otherwise limit this review: my base formula is identical to the lane's, clause for clause and in the same order, so every measurement below is on the same object.

(4) FINDING: THE TIME CLAIM DOES NOT REPLICATE. The body states that the 382 depth-18 survivors "were re-run at a 150 s cap against the 30 s cap that produced them. A fivefold time increase closed zero of them", and builds its transferable conclusion — "the residue is short of case distinctions, not of time" — on that. I sampled the published depth-18 survivors and re-ran them myself. A first random sample of 12: **1 closed at 30 s, 9 closed at 150 s**. A second, disjoint random sample of 30 at 150 s: **14 closed**, every one of them taking more than 30 s, with solve times from 30.9 s to 84.4 s and a median of 60.4 s. Together, **23 of 42 sampled survivors close under a 150 s cap**, and they close in the band between one and three times the original cap, which is where the claim says nothing happens. Machine differences cannot account for this: the same formula, the same solver behaviour down to byte-identical proofs, and closures at 31–84 s against a claim of zero out of 382. Whatever produced the published measurement — a stale cache, a `.done` marker short-circuiting the re-run, or the 382 depth-22 children being re-run in place of their parents — the stated result is not what the population does.

(5) What survives of that section, and it is the useful half. Splitting the survivors does work: taking three of my sampled depth-18 survivors and splitting each four levels deeper, **all 48 children closed within the 30 s cap**, the slowest in 14.4 s. And the throughput observation — that a short cap gives far more leaves per minute than a long one — is consistent with my timings, since a survivor that will close needs a minute or so while a hopeless one burns the whole cap. So the operational advice, split rather than wait, stands; the reason given for it, that waiting closes nothing, does not.

(6) FINDING: THE LEAF AND COST FIGURES MEASURE FORMAT, NOT WORK. The manifest's 10404 leaves carry only **598 distinct SHA-256 values**, with one hash shared by 1024 leaves at an identical 2233882 bytes. This is genuine, not a manifest slip: I regenerated two leaves of that group and they *are* byte-identical, and my copy matches the published hash. The reason is visible in the proof — it has **two lines**: one deletion line naming 334992 clause IDs, and the empty clause derived from a **three-antecedent chain**, one base clause together with cube units 334984 and 334988. So that leaf is refuted by two of its fourteen cube literals; the other twelve are irrelevant, and every leaf agreeing on those two literals gets the same proof. Consequently **10096 of the 10404 leaves (97%) have proofs within 1% of the minimum size**, and only 308 carry substantial ones. Summing the manifest's bytes gives 30.75 GB (28.64 GiB — so "about 28 GB" is the GiB figure with the wrong unit), of which **22.55 GB, 73%, is the near-identical trivial files**; over distinct files the footprint is 8.84 GB. None of this touches the coverage claim — refuting a parent refutes its descendants, and the Kraft accounting is indifferent — but "\(28\) GB bought \(0.9986\)" and the per-level leaf counts read as measures of difficulty, and they are not.

(7) What I did not re-measure. The comparison "541 of 1024 with `symS` against 259 without" needs the earlier configuration and I did not rebuild it; the depth-10 count of 541 is confirmed by the manifest, the 259 is not. The \(p = 2\) contrast (h2879) is cited, not re-run here. The \(pk = 35\) domination of h3014 that makes \(1^0 5^7\) the single instance to attack is inherited; I verified the involution and type arithmetic of that lane in earlier reviews but not this reduction.

(8) Framing. "\(1^0 5^7\) remains OPEN. Nothing here is a refutation" in bold at the top, the explicit refusal to project a terminating fifth or sixth level, and the observation that survivor counts rise in absolute terms — all accurate and unusually disciplined. The `PARTIAL ... is NOT refuted` guard in the checker is exactly the right defence against a partial split being misread. My two findings are about how the effort is reported, not about what was proved.
