Substituting into \(e(H) = P + e_H(A,R) + e(H[R])\) gives \(e(H[R]) = e(H) + P - \lvert A\rvert r + Y_A\), the boxed identity. The partition arithmetic is right as well: \(\lvert A\rvert + \lvert R\rvert = (\lvert C\rvert - 3 - s) + \lvert W\rvert + (s + 3 + b) = 58\), and \(X = 2m - 58 \cdot 28\) gives 52, 54, 56 at \(m = 838, 839, 840\) — the "of 52/54/56" the artifact prints. Additivity of the crossing number over the disjoint \(A\) and \(R\) is standard and correctly applied.

(5) THE FINDING: the dip is at \(Y_A = 47\). The body writes that the bound "dips to 7858 at \(Y_A = 48\), where only 4 units of excess remain on \(R\), 28 of its vertices are low, and Gallai forces a clique block of order 24". Those four facts are the \(Y_A = 48\) column, but there the Gallai route gives \(\mathrm{cr}(K_{26}) + \mathrm{cr}(K_{24}) = 4724 + 3357 = 8081\), which is what the bound is at that \(Y_A\) — above the dip and only 200 short of \(Z(29)\). The value 7858 belongs to \(Y_A = 47\), where 5 units of excess remain, 27 vertices are low, the forced block has order 23 and the Gallai route gives only 7521, so the dense route at 3134 binds. So the sentence pairs the right value with the wrong column. Nothing that the contribution concludes changes: the minimum of the profile is 7858 either way, "423 short" is \(8281 - 7858\) exactly, and the barrier is not closed. The lane's current head version has since softened this to "minimised at \(Y_A = 47\) or 48" with "a clique block of order 23 or 24", so the point appears to have been noticed later; the published body was not corrected.

(7) The two "points of care" are both right and both matter. Taking \(P\) as large as the feasibility cap allows rather than at its Turán cap is necessary because the bound decreases in \(P\): fixing \(P\) high would make feasible configurations look impossible, i.e. it is the direction that would produce a false closure, and the \(s = 0\) family is exactly where the cap binds. The cost of deleting a non-low vertex of \(R\), at most \(\min(\lvert R\rvert - 1, r)\) edges, follows from \(d_G = 28 + x_v\) and \(28k + k' \le 29k\) as stated.
