Row \((57,826)\) and the \(\lvert R\rvert = 7\) case of row \((57,827)\) close at 8343 against \(Z(29) = 8281\) only under the CCCG 2021 seeding; with the bare counting seed the same computation gives **8059**, and with the refereed McQuillan–Pan–Richter value **8122**, both below the threshold. (2) Ingredient A is sound, and the strengthening is real. Blocks of a graph are edge-disjoint and a crossing lies between two edges, so a crossing counted inside \(\mathrm{cr}(Q_i)\) is counted for no other block: \(\mathrm{cr}(G) \ge \sum_i \mathrm{cr}(Q_i)\) needs no vertex-disjointness, and the old restriction to blocks of order at least 15 was indeed unnecessary. The augmentation is right too: \(N_H(w_i) \subseteq B\) makes each \(w_i\) \(G\)-adjacent to every vertex of \(C\), \(w_1 w_2\) is a \(G\)-edge because the \(w_i\) are distinct components of \(H - B\), and the \(w_i\) are high so they lie in no block of \(G[L]\); hence \((Q_j \cap C) \cup \{w_1, w_2\}\) is a clique of order \(q_j - \beta_j + 2\) whose edges are disjoint from the other blocks', giving \(\mathrm{cr}(G) \ge \mathrm{cr}(K_{q_j - \beta_j + 2}) + \sum_{l \ne j} \mathrm{cr}(Q_l)\). The adversary's constraints — \(\beta_j \le 2\) because \(T\)'s vertices are pairwise \(G\)-non-adjacent so a clique holds at most one, at most one \(\beta_j = 2\) because only one block can hold \(s\), and \(\sum_j \beta_j \le 4\) because \(\lvert B\rvert = 4\) and a \(B\)-vertex in two blocks of order at least 22 would be a low vertex of degree at least 43 — all check.

(3) Ingredient B is sound, and it is not exposed to the audit now under way. The bound \(\sum_{\text{blocks} \ni v}(\lvert Q\rvert - 1) \ge 28 - \lvert R\rvert\) is Constraint C, whose consequences this lemma uses are "no isolated low vertex" and "a block with \(q - 1 < \delta_0\) has all its vertices in a second block, hence \(q \le \mathrm{extra}\)". Those are the \(\delta_0 \ge 1\) consequences, valid for \(\lvert R\rvert \le 27\). The lemma does **not** use the pairwise-disjointness of big blocks, which is the consequence researcher-2's current audit shows needs \(\lvert R\rvert \le 13\); so nothing here is affected by that audit, which I verified independently in an earlier pass.
