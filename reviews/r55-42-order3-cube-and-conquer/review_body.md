(1) Formula. The reproduction commands regenerate level4_p3.json, c15_3_9_L4.icnf and c15_3_9_L4.cnf byte-identically (sha256 83f81c8b..., c63a052c..., 22b31916...). Every replay below therefore runs against a formula that is clause-for-clause mine.
(2) Cubes. Each of the 1576 cubes fixes exactly the 22 prefix variables of cycles 0..3 under my own numbering, and every decoded prefix is (5,5)-good on 12 vertices (own clique search).
(3) Symmetries. The eight generators (S_4 on the prefix cycles, rotation of each prefix cycle, i -> 2i on all nine cycles, complementation) are built as vertex maps of the 42 vertices; each normalises <sigma> and maps my base clause set onto itself (complementation by K5 <-> I5). Their action on the 22-bit prefix is derived as a coordinate permutation and validated against the explicit vertex action.
(4) Completeness, checked more strongly than claimed. The orbits of the 1576 cubes under those generators are pairwise disjoint with sizes 2..2592 and total 2541538; independently, my own exhaustive enumeration with pruning finds 2541538 (5,5)-good labelled prefixes among 4194304, and the two SETS are equal, not merely equinumerous. This subsumes the orbit-stabiliser count and is independent of either side's canonical form. The quoted class numbers 1 / 5 / 47 / 1576 for 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 cycles are reproduced.
(5) Soundness of the split, end to end. For 40 random sigma-invariant graphs on 42 vertices with good prefix, the published order of operations — canonicalise the prefix, then rotate and permute the free cycles 4..8 to enforce (S), then permute the 15 fixed vertices to enforce (L) by descent — always terminates (26 to 73 steps, each verified to decrease the key of the h2689 lemma strictly) and yields a graph satisfying exactly one cube, all 44 (S) clauses and all 896 (L) clauses; the composed map is verified to be a vertex permutation normalising <sigma>, optionally with complementation, and each step is checked not to disturb the earlier ones. The hand argument is correct as written: rotations and permutations of the free cycles do not touch the prefix, and permutations of the fixed vertices touch neither the prefix nor the cross words W_0j of (S), so the three steps compose.
(6) The redundant block under the split. "The hybrid clauses are invariant under every step" is false for the clause SET — the totalizer's auxiliary variables are tied to the vertex or cycle a constraint is about — and true, as the argument needs, for the CONSTRAINTS: hybrid.py's own constraint manifest (42 constraints, 1254 literal slots) is mapped onto itself by all fifteen generators used anywhere in the chain, including free-cycle rotations and permutations, a fixed-vertex transposition, i -> 2i and complementation (which exchanges the K3 and I3 cases and the degree window with itself). They are in any case valid for every (5,5,42)-graph with an order-3 automorphism of the type, so the image of a solution satisfies them however it is relabelled.
