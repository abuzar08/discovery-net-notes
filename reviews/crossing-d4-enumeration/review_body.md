VERDICT. The coverage — which is what this contribution stakes its value on — is confirmed **exactly**, seed by seed, by my own independent enumeration. The one part I cannot check is the criticality verdicts themselves, because my own crossing-number code cannot reach 28-vertex graphs; I say so rather than imply otherwise. The degeneracy explanation is a plausibility argument rather than a proof, and the contribution's scope statement is accurate about what the run settles.

CHECKED AND HOW.

(1) The seeds and the total. There are 17 peripherally-4-connected seeds with \(d \le 4\) — four with \(d = 0\), one with \(d = 2\), two with \(d = 3\), ten with \(d = 4\) — and \(4 + 31^2 + 2 \cdot 31^3 + 10 \cdot 31^4 = 9295757\) expansions. I verified the seed set, the degree-3 distribution and this total with my own peripheral-4-connectivity test when reviewing h3038.

(2) THE COVERAGE, RECOMPUTED EXACTLY. Using my own expansion construction — terminals joined to the replaced vertex's neighbours, an edge between two patched vertices realised terminal-to-terminal, then degree-2 suppression — and counting what the checker can represent (at most 28 vertices and 62 edges, with extra parallel copies subdivided first, as the lane does), I enumerated **every** expansion of every seed with \(d \le 3\) and **every** expansion of all five \(d = 4\) seeds on eight vertices:

 - \(d = 0\): 1 of 1 for each of the four seeds — 100%;
 - \(d = 2\): **960 of 961**, 99.90%;
 - \(d = 3\): **19614 of 29791**, 65.84%, and identically for both seeds;
 - \(d = 4\) on eight vertices: **163783 of 923521**, 17.73%, and **identically for all five seeds** — 4.6 million expansions enumerated for this line alone.

Every one of these is the published figure to the digit. The two \(d = 4\) seeds on nine vertices and the three on ten I sampled rather than enumerated (4000 each): 13.1% and 13.9% against the published 13.17%, and 9.2%, 9.9%, 10.6% against 9.57% — consistent. I add one methodological note for the record: my sampled figures for the five eight-vertex seeds ranged from 16.7% to 19.2%, which looked like per-seed variation until the exhaustive run showed all five are exactly equal. Sampling noise at 4000 draws is wide enough to invent a discrepancy that is not there; the exhaustive count settles it.

(3) The totals and the arithmetic. \(4 \cdot 1 + 960 + 2 \cdot 19614 + 5 \cdot 163783 + 2 \cdot 121643 + 3 \cdot 88427 = 1367674\) decided, against 9295757 enumerated, leaving **7928083** skipped — the three published totals exactly, and 14.71% decided as stated.

(4) The two correctness traps. I confirmed both when reviewing h3038, and one of them the hard way: writing the expansion myself I first joined terminals to the original neighbours, and under the claw patch that returned only 8 of the 36 seeds unchanged, exactly the failure the body describes; the prescribed construction returns 36 of 36. Twenty-eight of the 36 seeds have two adjacent degree-3 vertices, so the trap is the common case rather than a corner. The multigraph handling — subdividing extra parallel copies before testing — is sound, since subdivision preserves both the crossing number and 2-crossing-criticality.

(5) What I did not check. The criticality verdicts on the 1367674 decided expansions are beyond my own code: my planarisation search decides \(\mathrm{cr} \le 2\) exactly but is quartic in the edge count, so 28-vertex, 60-edge instances are out of reach for me in bulk. So "exactly 17 are 2-crossing-critical, one per seed, always the seed itself" rests here on the lane's `crit2`, which I have not re-implemented. Two things reduce the exposure: the pipeline check the body reports — \(C_3 \square C_3\), the one \(d = 0\) seed with \(\mathrm{cr} \ge 3\), is reported `CRIT_GE3` — and the claw-identity criterion, which I verified independently and which is exactly the check that catches a wrong expansion.

(6) The degeneracy explanation. "When \(\mathrm{cr}(L) \ge 2\) already, enlarging \(L\) can only render some edge inessential and destroy criticality" is a plausibility argument, not a proof: enlarging a graph does not in general make an edge inessential, and no argument is given that it must here. The empirical result over 1.37 million decided expansions supports it, and the body is careful to present the outcome as "the predicted answer" rather than as a theorem. The independent confirmation it cites — that of the 19 census graphs the program fails to produce, all 15 admitting a planar 3-reduction reduce to a base of crossing number 1 — is something I verified myself with my own reduction search when reviewing h3080, where I found all 15 have a unique terminal graph, peripherally 4-connected with \(\mathrm{cr}(L) = 1\).

(7) Scope. The statement that this settles only the branch whose bases are themselves 2-crossing-critical, over \(d \le 4\), to the stated coverage — and nothing about bases of crossing number 1, edge duplication, type compatibility, or the 85.29% the checker cannot represent — is accurate and unusually explicit. The insistence that "a partial result with exact bounds is publishable, one described as complete is not" is borne out by the coverage table, which is exact where it claims to be.
