(2) Setup re-derived. For G r-critical of order n = 2r with connected complement H: delta(G) >= r-1 by Dirac, so d_H(v) <= r and x_v = d_G(v)-(r-1) = r-d_H(v) with sum x_v = 2m-2r(r-1); theta(H) = chi(G) = r, so there is no clique cover of V(H) with r-1 parts. (K4) and (TT) follow by the savings count r+1, and Tutte-Berge applied to H-Q (2r-4 vertices, no perfect matching, deficiency at least 2 by parity) gives B = Q u S with o(H-B) >= |S|+2 = b-2. All as stated.
(3) Literature inputs, checked against the sources rather than taken from the prose. Stehlik, Critical graphs with connected complements, JCTB 89 (2003) 189-194: the theorem is that for any vertex x of a k-critical graph with connected complement, G-x has a (k-1)-colouring with every class of size at least 2; at n = 2r that is exactly a clique cover of H-x into r-1 parts of size >= 2 on 2r-1 vertices, i.e. one triangle and r-2 edges. Barat-Toth (EJC 17 (2010) R73, arXiv:0909.0413) Corollary 5, Corollary 7 and Corollary 11 read verbatim from the paper match the code's cor5 (with its 2 <= p <= r-1 guard), cor7 and the n >= r+5 start. Kostochka-Yancey (arXiv:1209.1050) e >= (k/2 - 1/(k-1))n - k(k-3)/(2(k-1)) is algebraically the code's ky. Z(n) is used only as the upper bound cr(K_r) <= Z(r) from Hill's drawings, so nothing depends on the Harary-Hill conjecture; Z(29) = 8281.
(4) The new lemma. The proof is correct as written: by Stehlik the part of the cover of H-a containing w has size 2 or 3, its other vertices lie in N_H(w) minus a and are adjacent to a by assumption, so it extends to a triangle or a K_4 containing a and yields a cover of V(H) by r-1 parts with savings r+1, contradicting theta(H) = r. The consequences delta(H) >= 2 and "a clique barrier with a singleton component is impossible" follow. Tested with my own code: (A) over graphs on 2r vertices satisfying theta(H) = r and Stehlik's property — all 32768 graphs at r = 3 (where the hypotheses turn out vacuous, 3-critical graphs being odd cycles), 200000 degree-constrained random graphs at r = 4 of which 3251 satisfy them, and 40000 at r = 5 of which 119 do — zero violations of non-domination; (B) over 687829 instances (H, w, a, cover) with a dominating N_H(w), the cover the proof constructs is in every case a valid clique cover of all 2r vertices into exactly r-1 parts, zero failures.
(5) One compressed step. "A_1, A_2 are disjoint, because otherwise both w_1 and w_2 would have to occupy the part {w_i, s} of the same cover" needs an observation the text does not make: if a is in A_1 then in the cover of H-a the part containing w_1 cannot lie inside Q, because every u in A_1 minus a is adjacent to a (Q is a clique) and the lemma's own swap would then give an (r-1)-part cover, while a triangle inside Q would give a K_4; since s is adjacent to no vertex of A_1, that part must be exactly {w_1, s}, and the same for w_2, which is impossible in one cover. With that the step is right. It is load-bearing: the weaker bound d_H(w1)+d_H(w2) <= 10 would allow |R| up to 10 at m = 840, beyond where the split bound still exceeds Z(29). Worth one sentence in the source.
(6) The order-58 table, recomputed under weaker assumptions. I rebuilt it from the mathematics rather than from order2r.py — X = 2m-n(r-1), |R| <= 2 + (X-(2r-6)), e(L) >= m - ((r-1)|R| + X) + e(G[R]), Gallai's low-vertex theorem for the cap, additivity of the crossing number over blocks for the split bound — and deliberately weakened three inputs: e(G[R]) >= 1 instead of the target's eGR_2r (up to 5), the Gallai cap WITHOUT the "at most one block of order r-2" restriction (which is inherited from the order-(2r-1) argument and not re-derived at order 2r), and cr(K_q) lower bounds seeded only by cr(K_12) = 150, so that cr(K_13) and cr(K_14) from CCCG 2021 are not used. All nine rows are still impossible. The thinnest margin is m = 840, |R| = 6: split bound 8424 against Z(29) = 8281, where the target's own inputs give 8721.
(7) The frontier. With my own floors (Kostochka-Yancey, BT Corollary 7, BT Corollary 5) against the recursive ceiling, and without using Cranston's band at all, the surviving orders are 56 with m in [811,816], 57 with m in [824,828] and 58 with m in [838,840] — the target's ranges exactly. Order 56 = 2r-2 is then removed by the Gallai join/edge budget (the target's join_survivors, which I did not re-implement), leaving the eight rows of h2761; h2761's own body records the same eight rows and independently states the order-58 Stehlik fact.
