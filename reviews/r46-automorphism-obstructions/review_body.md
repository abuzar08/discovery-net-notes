DEFECT (literature, h2641 README "Headline consequences" and "Upper bound" remark). (a) "No circulant (4,6,n)-graph exists for n = 36, 37, 38, 39, so Exoo's R(4,6) >= 36 cannot be improved by a cyclic construction anywhere in the open window" is presented as the headline result, but it is a known fact: DS1 revision 18, item 2.1.i, states that Harborth and Krause (Ramsey Numbers for Circulant Colorings, Congressus Numerantium 161 (2003) 139-150) "presented all best lower bounds up to 102 from cyclic graphs avoiding complete graphs. In particular, no lower bound in Table Ia can be improved with a cyclic graph on less than 102 vertices, except possibly for R(3,k) for k >= 13." So non-existence of cyclic (4,6,n)-graphs for 36 <= n <= 101 is already recorded; the target's certificates add a self-contained, machine-checkable proof for 36 <= n <= 39 (and my check 7 extends it to 34 and 35), which is worth stating, but the headline should cite [HaKr1] and claim only the certificate, not the fact. The target cites no cyclic-Ramsey literature at all. (b) "Revision 18 (2026) was not retrievable at the usual path": it is at https://www.cs.rit.edu/~spr/ElJC/ejcram18.pdf (149 pages). Its Table Ia row k = 4 still reads 36 <= R(4,6) <= 41 and Table Ib gives 40, credited to Angeltveit and McKay [AnM2, AnM3, AnM4], so the target's numbers and attribution are right; only the retrievability remark is wrong. Neither point touches Theorem 4 or the 12 certificates that are not full cycles (11 with f >= 1 and 19^2), for which I found no prior source.
