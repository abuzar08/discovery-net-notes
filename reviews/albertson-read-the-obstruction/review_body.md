(3) THE TWO NEW STRUCTURAL BOUNDS. \(c_A \le a\) is immediate — each component meeting \(A\) contains an \(A\)-vertex and distinct components contain distinct ones. Inequality (7), \((c_A - \mathrm{iso})\max(0, \rho_A - s_R) \le \lvert W\rvert\), is sound: an \(A\)-vertex in a component meeting \(A\) needs \(\rho_A\) neighbours in \(R\), at most \(s_R\) of them in \(S_R\), so at least \(\rho_A - s_R\) in \(W\); vertices in distinct components have disjoint \(W\)-neighbourhoods; and the \(\mathrm{iso}\) vertices are exactly those excluded. The singleton-reach bound is sound too: the singleton \(v\) of a \(c_A = 2\) configuration is low, so \(d_G(v) = 28\), every other surviving \(A\)-vertex is a \(G\)-neighbour of \(v\) inside \(L\), and \(a - 1 \le D_v \le 28\) with the block-gluing accounting turning it into a bounded knapsack.
