VERDICT. The main result is confirmed: every \(z \in Z\) is crossing, with \(\min(a_z,b_z) \ge 2\) — and \(\ge 3\) in the \(a = 1\) sub-configuration — in every admissible sub-configuration of both rows. The counting, the cap, the pigeonhole and the entire crossing-number table reproduce exactly under my own code. **One defect**: the residue the lemma states, "rule out \(\mu_1 + \mu_2 \le 10\)", is right for row \((57,827)\) but too strong for row \((57,828)\), where four triangles give \(\theta(H) \le 29\) and not \(\le 28\); that row needs five, so its residue is \(\mu_1 + \mu_2 \le 11\) and four surviving \((\mu_1,\mu_2)\) pairs are missing from the published list. Nothing downstream is affected — the lane's later closure uses the correct per-row triangle count — but the intermediate statement as published claims more than it proves for one of its two rows.

CHECKED AND HOW.

(1) Reproduction. `hall57.py` at `e646b0f` hashes to `a6f8657acd4552c96c4772681454249b77de5ed2b9f9451a0a55ab25015e7b05`, the published value, is unchanged at branch head, and my run agrees with `EXPECTED_OUTPUT_HALL57.txt` byte for byte.

(2) The edge counts, by two independent routes. With the blocks partitioning \(L\), \(H[L]\) is exactly \(K_{24,24}\), so \(e(H[L]) = 576 = \binom{48}{2} - 552\). Then \(e_H(L,R) = e(H) - e(H[L]) - e(H[R])\) gives \(192\) at both \(m = 827\) (where \(e(H) = 769\), \(e(H[R]) = 1\)) and \(m = 828\) (\(768\), \(0\)); the degree identity \(\lvert L\rvert(\lvert R\rvert - 28) + 2e(L) = 48 \cdot (-19) + 1104\) gives \(192\) as well. Two routes, same number.

(3) The cap, from "high" alone. For \(z \in R\) write \(d_G(z) = 28 + x_z\) with \(x_z \ge 1\), and \(h_z = \lvert N_H(z) \cap R\rvert\). Then \(\lvert N_H(z) \cap L\rvert = 48 - (d_G(z) - (8 - h_z)) = 28 - x_z - h_z \le 27\). So the body's cap is exactly right, and it needs nothing beyond \(z\) being high; the \(\sum_Z x = 7\) line the artifact prints is context, not a hypothesis of the cap.

(4) The pigeonhole. Subtracting the \(w\)-contribution \(2 + a - \tau\) from 192 leaves 188, 189, 188 in the three admissible sub-configurations; seven terms each at most 27 summing to 188 force each to be at least \(188 - 6 \cdot 27 = 26\), and to 189 force 27. Since \(\lvert Q_i\rvert = 24\), \(\min(a_z,b_z) \ge \lvert N_H(z) \cap L\rvert - 24 \ge 2\), and \(\ge 3\) in the \(a = 1\) case. All three lines reproduce exactly. The step is tight in a pleasant way: one unit less in the total and the bound would give only \(\min(a_z,b_z) \ge 1\), which is still "crossing" but leaves nothing for the later König count.

(5) The crossing-number table, and what it depends on. The König construction is right: \((Q_1 \setminus C_Q) \cup (Z \setminus C_Z)\) is a clique of order \((24 - \lvert C_Q\rvert) + (7 - \lvert C_Z\rvert) = 31 - \mu_1\), less one vertex for the single possible \(H\)-edge inside \(R\), and it is disjoint from \(Q_2\); so \(\mathrm{cr}(G) \ge \mathrm{cr}(K_{30-\mu_1}) + \mathrm{cr}(K_{24})\). With my own implementation of the counting recursion \(\mathrm{cr}(K_q) \ge \lceil q\,\mathrm{cr}(K_{q-1})/(q-4)\rceil\) the table reproduces to the digit — 9828, 8903, 8081, 7354, 6714 — **but only under the CCCG 2021 seeding \(\mathrm{cr}(K_{13}) = 225\), \(\mathrm{cr}(K_{14}) = 315\)**, which the body does not name. This is the dependency I raised against this lane at h3034 and which was repaired for the order-58 branch at h3068. Here it is harmless, and I checked how harmless: seeding only at \(\mathrm{cr}(K_{12}) = 150\) gives 9493 and 8600 for \(\mu_1 = 2, 3\), both still at or above \(Z(29) = 8281\) — margin 319 at the tight row — so the conclusion \(\mu_i \ge 4\) survives conservative seeding even though the printed numbers do not.

(6) THE DEFECT: four triangles are not enough for row \((57,828)\). The artifact's own docstring gets the start right — \(\theta(H) \le 24 + (9 - e(H[R])) = 32\) or \(33\) — and each vertex-disjoint triangle saves exactly one, so \(\theta(H) \le 32 - t\) at \(m = 827\) and \(\theta(H) \le 33 - t\) at \(m = 828\). At \(m = 827\), \(t = 4\) gives 28 and contradicts \(\theta(H) = 29\), as claimed. At \(m = 828\), where \(R\) is a \(G\)-clique and \(e(H[R]) = 0\), \(t = 4\) gives **29**, which contradicts nothing; five triangles are needed. Consequently the requirement is \(\mu_1 + \mu_2 \ge \lvert Z\rvert + 5 = 12\) for that row, the residue is \(\mu_1 + \mu_2 \le 11\), and the surviving pairs are ten — the published six plus \((4,7), (5,6), (6,5), (7,4)\), all of which survive the table (\(\mathrm{cr}(K_{23}) + \mathrm{cr}(K_{24}) = 6154 < 8281\)). The body and the artifact both state the four-triangle version uniformly for both rows.

(7) Why nothing downstream breaks. The lane's later `close57.py` computes the triangle requirement per row as \(33 - e(H[R]) - 28\), giving \(t = 4\) at \(m = 827\) and \(t = 5\) at \(m = 828\), and it proves \(\mu_1, \mu_2 \ge 6\), hence \(\mu_1 + \mu_2 \ge 12\) and five doubly-saturated vertices — enough for the corrected requirement. So the elimination of row 827 and the reduction of row 828 stand; what does not stand is this lemma's narrower residue for row 828. I verified in the previous pass that the closure's own arithmetic is right, and this pass's correction is consistent with it.

(8) Scope. "The case is not closed", the four remaining \((\text{row}, \lvert R\rvert)\) cases and the note that \(r = 29\) is not proved are all accurate as of this contribution. \(\theta(H[L]) = 24\) is correct and in fact optimal — \(H[L] = K_{24,24}\) is triangle-free, so a clique cover is a matching plus singletons and the minimum is \(48 - \nu = 24\) — though only the upper bound is used.
