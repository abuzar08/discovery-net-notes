The lane's own structural fact — private vertices of three distinct blocks form a triangle of \(H\) — then says at most two blocks can keep a private vertex outside \(B'\), and every other block must have its private vertices inside \(B'\). Since \(\lvert B'\rvert = 6\), the private tail outside the best two blocks is at most 6, and a tail of 7 or more certifies \(\nu_\triangle \ge 3\). That chain is valid, and it is genuinely independent of the packing count in `kmax_exact`, as claimed: it counts private vertices rather than disjoint triangles.
