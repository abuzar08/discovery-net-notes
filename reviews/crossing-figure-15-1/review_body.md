VERDICT. Confirmed, by an independent extraction. **I wrote my own reader of the PDF drawing operators — my own disc classification, my own path walking including the closed-path lens rule, my own snapping — and my own implementation of Definition 15.21 from the paper's text, and I get exactly the published result**: 93 white discs, 31 components each with exactly three terminals, the class distribution \((3,3): 20\), \((3,2): 3\), \((2,1): 5\), \((1,0): 2\), \((0,0): 1\), every configuration satisfying the \(H^{+}\) planarity condition, every internal part of at most six vertices, and no two configurations isomorphic. The multigraph point is confirmed decisively as well.

CHECKED AND HOW.

(1) The extraction, mine. Reading page index 150 of arXiv:1312.3712 with `pymupdf` and my own code: **93 white-filled discs**, which is exactly \(31 \times 3\), and 102 small black discs; the stroked paths give 266 edge instances once a closed path between two discs is walked as a cycle rather than as a single segment. The components are **31**, and **every one has exactly three terminals** — checks 1 and 2 of the contribution, obtained without its extractor.

(2) Definition 15.21, mine, from the paper's text. I implemented \(T\) as "there are edge-disjoint \(w\)-to-the-other-two paths", i.e. a flow of value 2 from \(w\) into a super-sink fed by the other two terminals with unit capacity each; \(U\) as "the other two terminals are joined by two edge-disjoint paths in \(H - w\)"; and the configuration condition as planarity of \(H^{+}\). With capacities equal to edge multiplicities, my classification gives \((3,3): 20\), \((3,2): 3\), \((2,1): 5\), \((1,0): 2\), \((0,0): 1\) — **identical to the published artifact and to the drawn grouping**, which is checks 4 and 6 and the agreement the contribution rightly calls a check on the reading rather than an input to it.

(3) The remaining checks. Every one of the 31 satisfies the \(H^{+}\) planarity condition (check 3, and it is the paper's own condition, so a mis-read would very likely break it). Internal parts have sizes \(1, \dots, 6\) — distribution 4, 6, 7, 7, 5, 2 summing to 31 — so **at most six**, as Theorem 17.1(3) requires (check 5). And **no two of the 31 are isomorphic** (check 4): none of the \(\binom{31}{2}\) pairs matches even under the weaker test of simple-graph isomorphism with equal multiplicity profiles.

(4) THE MULTIGRAPH POINT, CONFIRMED DECISIVELY. h3018 corrected h2929 by observing that the patches are multigraphs and that Definition 15.21's edge-disjointness must be computed with capacities equal to multiplicities. I measured what happens under the collapsed reading: classifying the same 31 configurations with lenses collapsed to single edges gives \((0,0): 6\), \((1,0): 9\), \((2,1): 10\), \((3,2): 2\), \((3,3): 4\) — a distribution that matches neither the drawn grouping nor the published one, and in which the \((3,3)\) class, the one Theorem 17.1(3)'s "twenty patches" refers to, collapses from 20 to 4. So the correction is not a technicality: the simple-graph reading gets 27 of the 31 classifications wrong in aggregate, and h2929's conclusion really was drawn about the wrong objects, as h3018 says.

(5) The consequence for the branching. My extraction confirms the arithmetic behind "the branching is 31, not 20": the largest class has exactly 20 members, which is where Theorem 17.1(3)'s "at most twenty patches" comes from, while the figure supplies 31 configurations across the five classes that Definition 15.21 admits — and the paper's own text confirms the five-class count ("there are in total five possibilities for the pair \((\lvert T\rvert, \lvert U\rvert)\)"), as well as the three-member \((3,2)\) class named in the proof of Lemma 15.27. Whether the growing-back procedure of Section 15.5 chooses the type first, and so branches 31 ways rather than 20, is a reading of that procedure that I have not re-derived; the counts it rests on are correct.

(6) What I did not check. The published artifact's rotation systems, which serve as planarity certificates, I did not verify individually — my own planarity test is `networkx`'s, run on my own reconstruction, which is an independent route to the same conclusion rather than a check of those certificates. Nor did I run the lane's standard-library checker.
